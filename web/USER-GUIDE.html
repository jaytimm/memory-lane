How to use Memory Lane

Memory Lane does not change or delete photographs. Everything stays on the USB drive.

  1. Browse an album.Click an album picture. Its photographs appear below, 100 at a time.
  2. Search for something you remember.Type a short description such as yellow tents, hugs, or two smiling faces, then click Search. You will see the 50 closest matches.
  3. See a random selection.Leave the description empty and click Search to see 50 random photographs. Click it again for a different selection.
  4. Narrow by decade.Every decade starts ticked. Untick All years, tick the decades you want, then click Search.
  5. See search ideas.Open the examples below the search box. Clicking one puts it in the search box; then click Search.
  6. Open a photograph.Click it to enlarge it. Use the left and right arrows to move through the results.
  7. Find related photographs.Open a photograph and choose Find similar photos or Find similar faces.
  8. What search can and can’t do.Memory Lane doesn’t know anyone’s name, so searching for “Grandma” or “Joe” won’t work. Every search works by how things look. Typed search finds photographs that look like your words, such as birthday cake or people at a lake. Find similar photos finds photographs that look like the one you opened. Find similar faces finds faces that look like the one you chose; it can include relatives who resemble each other and miss the same person at a very different age.
  9. Open, copy, or save the original.Open a photograph and choose Go to original to fit the full-resolution photograph to your screen. You can also right-click it and use your browser’s normal Copy image or Save image as command. These are built-in browser commands; Memory Lane does not change the photograph.
  10. Start over.Click Clear search. It empties the search box and ticks every decade again.
